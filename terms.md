# Lunchbox quotation terms

- The minimum order is 20 boxes.
- The price per box depends on the total number of boxes in the order, and that one price applies to every box. The prices and delivery fees are in rates-lunchbox.json.
- GST of 9% is charged on the boxes plus the delivery fee.
- A quotation states the boxes, price per box, delivery fee, GST and total payable. Nothing else is offered.
- There are no discounts, free items or special offers unless they appear in rates-lunchbox.json.
- Once a customer accepts a quotation, Heicoders Catering honours the quoted price.
